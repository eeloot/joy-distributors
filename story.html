HOW THEY STARTED

It began with a single pot of rice. Amina and Leila asked local vendors for leftover ingredients. Within a week, they had organized three rotating cooking teams with help from friends and teachers.

CHALLENGES THEY FACED

Securing consistent funding and navigating logistical hurdles were major obstacles early on. Sourcing fresh produce during the off-season required building new relationships with neighboring communities and establishing a local food bank network.

WHAT CHANGED

The initiative brought the community together. School attendance increased by 40% because children were guaranteed a warm meal. Parents also began volunteering, creating a stronger sense of shared responsibility and unity.

WHAT'S NEXT

Our immediate goal is to establish a permanent community kitchen by the end of the year. We are also looking to launch a vocational training program to teach sustainable agriculture and culinary skills to local youth.
